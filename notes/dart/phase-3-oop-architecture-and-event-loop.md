## 📋 Curriculum Overview
- **Constructors Deep-Dive**: Generative, Initializer lists, Redirecting, Factory constructors, and `const` canonicalization.

- **Generics**: Generic types, methods, type bounds, and `covariant`.
- **The Event Loop & Async Dart**: Microtask Queue vs Event Queue, `Future`, `Completer`, Single-subscription vs Broadcast `Stream`, and subscription leak prevention.
